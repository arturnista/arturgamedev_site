Time Dungeon

You wake in a mysterious dungeon and must find a way out. Every action consumes actions; when they run out, your health starts to fall. Run out of health and you die.

Find items, fight enemies, grow stronger, and escape the dungeon.

Selected as one of Newgrounds’ Top 10 Best Games of February 2022.

Controls WASD: Move · E: Pick up equipment and items · Space: Use your item

Play on Newgrounds ↗
