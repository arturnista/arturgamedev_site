# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Game studios and recruiters evaluating Artur Nista's game-development work and professional experience.

## Product Purpose

Present Artur Nista's games, skills, and professional experience so visitors can assess his work, play the games, or contact him.

## Positioning

The portfolio brings playable and published independent game projects together with hands-on professional game-development experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Visitors can browse game descriptions and screenshots, follow external play and social links, and review career history and skills.
- Several projects are playable through external platforms or hosted browser builds.
- Preserve the existing game catalog, game links, social links, and career history; do not invent new projects or credentials.

## Evidence on Hand

- Homepage copy and project links: `public/index.html`.
- Game screenshots and profile photograph: `public/img/`.
- Hosted browser game builds: `public/games/`.
- Professional experience and skills are stated on the homepage; no testimonials or quantified performance claims are provided.

## Product Principles

- Make the work easy to inspect and play.
- Keep professional experience and skills easy to assess.
- Preserve accurate project details and outbound links.
- Make contacting Artur straightforward.
